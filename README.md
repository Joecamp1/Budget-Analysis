# 📊 Budget-Analysis
This repo a quick analysis of the important and non-important expenses of the National Budget

## 🛠 Tools used 
- Microsoft Excel
- PowerBi
- Google Sheets

## 📈 Project Overview
This project analyzes the Nigerian National budget of 2025.
An interactive Power BI report that breaks down the Nigerian federal budget by Ministries, Departments and Agencies (MDAs). It shows how total allocation splits across Capital, Overhead and Personnel spending, which MDAs receive the most, and how spending differs between core and non-core functions.

The report has four pages: a national overview, plus deep dives into the Presidency, the Ministry of Defence and the Ministry of Finance.

## ✅ Key insights
1. The budget totals about 46T, split roughly 13T capital, 19T overhead and 14T personnel. Overhead is the largest component.
2. Spending is overwhelmingly core. Core functions take about 42T (91.3%) of the total, and non-core functions about 4T (8.7%).
3. The Ministry of Finance is the largest single allocation at about 18T, roughly 40% of the national total. Its allocation is dominated by overhead (about 93%), and the Debt Management Office is by far its biggest line.
4. Recurrent costs dominate the smaller bodies. Personnel makes up about 77% of the Presidency's allocation (356bn of 465bn) and about 71% of the Ministry of Defence's.
5. The Ministry of Defence is personnel-heavy. Its capital allocation (582bn) is much smaller than its personnel cost (2T), with the Nigerian Army taking the largest share.
6. Allocation is highly concentrated. On the national page, the top MDA dwarfs the rest of the Top 10, which tail off sharply after the first two or three bars.

>Note: values are shown as rounded in the visuals (T = trillion, bn = billion), so small differences between totals and components are due to rounding.

## 🗂️ Data
Source: [Budget Office Appropriation Bill](https://budgetoffice.gov.ng/index.php/2025-executive-proposal/2025-executive-proposal/viewdocument/986)
Fiscal year: 2025
Currency: Naira (₦)
Key fields: MDA, Function (Core / Non-core), Capital, Overhead, Personnel, Total Allocation

## 📝 Report features
- Four report pages with consistent layout and a dark theme
- KPI cards for Capital, Overhead, Personnel and Total Allocation
- Top 10 MDAs by total allocation on every page
- Donut charts for spending mix and Core vs Non-core split
- MDA lists and tables for drilling into individual agencies
